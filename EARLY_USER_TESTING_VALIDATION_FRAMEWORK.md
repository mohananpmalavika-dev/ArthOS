## 📋 The Early User-Testing Validation Framework

When you put this prototype in front of your first small cohort (e.g., 20–30 users), your primary goal is to capture baseline data that traditional fintech ignores. You want to track three core validation pillars:

### 1. The Reality Gap (Psychology vs. Math)

- **What to watch:** Compare the **Perceived Runway** against the **Actual Cash Runway**.
- **The Hypothesis:** If your aggregated serverless telemetry shows that the average user's *Perceived Runway* is significantly higher than their *Actual Cash Runway*, you have mathematically proven the "Visibility Blindspot." This is a powerful data point to show future investors and technical partners that users have a critical need for an intelligence layer.

### 2. Behavioral Triggers as Leading Risk Indicators

- **What to watch:** Look at the correlation between high **Future Risk Exposure** labels and low **Behaviour Scores**.
- **The Hypothesis:** You will likely find that users with "High Risk" or "Critical Risk" flags aren't failing because their income is low, but because their scores are dragged down by structural behavioral triggers—such as overspending when stressed or lacking an impulse waiting rule. This directly validates the founder's note: *human behavior drives every financial decision, and systems must catch it before collapse happens*.

### 3. Action Receptivity

- **What to watch:** When users reach the end of the daily habits wizard step, track how they react to the **Recommended Action** section.
- **The Hypothesis:** Since the app now cleanly displays a path to immediately change their baseline (such as shifting from an As-Is Lifestyle runway to a Crisis Mode Optimized runway), you can follow up with your test group to see if that visual contrast successfully motivated them to implement the recommended action.
