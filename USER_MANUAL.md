## 1. What is ARTH.OS?

ARTH.OS is a **Financial Health Assessment tool** that combines behavioral psychology with financial data to give you a clear picture of your money health — not just numbers, but the behaviors, habits, and blind spots that drive them.

### What ARTH.OS does:

- Gives you a **Health Score (0–100)** based on your financial behavior, awareness, and stability
- Calculates **how many months you can survive** if your income stops today
- Identifies your **money personality archetype** (Reactor, Survivor, Planner, or Builder)
- Reveals your **financial blind spots** — where your perception differs from reality
- Recommends **one specific action** you should take next
- Lets you **simulate purchases** before you make them to see the impact on your runway

### What ARTH.OS does NOT do:

- It does **not** connect to your bank account
- It does **not** track your transactions automatically
- It does **not** store your personal identity (name, email, IP address) in analytics

Everything you enter stays in your browser until you choose to save or export.

## 3. The Assessment Wizard

The assessment is a **4-step guided wizard**. You'll see a progress bar at the top showing your current step.

### Step 1: Psychology (Behaviour)

**12 questions** about your emotional relationship with money. Examples:

- How emotionally connected are you to money?
- Do social environments influence your spending?
- How often do you make unplanned purchases?
- Do you avoid checking your balance during stress?
- When you're bored or stressed, how likely are you to spend?
- Do you review subscriptions regularly?

**How to answer:** Select the option that best describes your typical behavior. There are no right or wrong answers — the system scores your responses to build your behaviour profile.

### Step 2: Clarity (Awareness)

**8 questions** about how well you know your finances. Examples:

- Do you track your monthly expenses?
- Do you know your total debt?
- Do you know your savings rate?
- Do you revisit your budget each month?
- Do you know your top 3 expense categories?

**How to answer:** Be honest about your tracking habits. The gap between what you *think* you know and what the math says is your blind spot.

### Step 3: Resilience (Stability)

**Numeric inputs + 4 categorical questions** about your financial cushion.

| Field | What to Enter |
|-------|---------------|
| Monthly expenses | Your average monthly spending (₹) |
| Fixed emergency buffer | Money reserved for essential survival (₹) |
| Discretionary emergency buffer | Extra savings beyond essentials (₹) |
| Total debt | All outstanding loans and credit (₹) |
| Monthly income | Your average monthly income (₹) |
| Fixed commitments | Rent, EMI, insurance — fixed outflows (₹) |
| Income stability | How consistent your income is |
| Dependents | How many people rely on your income |
| Debt repayment rate | What % of income goes to debt repayment |
| Average interest rate | Approximate annual interest on your debt |

**Tip:** Enter amounts in Indian Rupees (₹). Use approximate values if you're unsure — the system works with estimates.

### Step 4: Habits (v2 only)

**2 questions** about your money routines:

- How many times per week do you do a quick money check-in?
- How reliably do you follow through on debt payments?

### Navigation

- **Previous button:** Go back one step (disabled on Step 1)
- **Continue button:** Advance to the next step
- **Finish & Review Score:** On the last step, this button completes your assessment and shows results

**Your progress is saved automatically.** If you close the tab and come back, you'll resume at the step you left off.

## 4. Understanding Your Results

### The Result Panel (Right Side)

As you fill in the assessment, your results update **in real time** on the right side of the screen (or below on mobile).

### Health Score (0–100)

Your overall financial health, broken into four bands:

| Score | Band | What It Means |
|-------|------|---------------|
| 0–25 | **Critical** | Immediate action needed. Your financial foundation needs urgent attention. |
| 26–50 | **Vulnerable** | You have some buffers but are exposed to shocks. Strengthen your weakest area. |
| 51–75 | **Stable** | You're on solid ground. Focus on closing awareness gaps and building reserves. |
| 76–100 | **Healthy** | Strong financial position. Maintain discipline and watch for blind spots. |

### Component Breakdown

Your Health Score is made of three components:

1. **Behaviour (0–45):** Your spending psychology — impulse control, emotional triggers, social influence
2. **Awareness (0–30):** How well you track and know your finances
3. **Stability (0–25):** Your financial cushion — savings, debt burden, income consistency

The **lowest component** drives your recommended action.

### Survival Estimate

**"If your income stopped today, how many months could you survive?"**

You'll see three numbers:

- **As-Is Lifestyle:** Based on your current monthly expenses
- **Crisis Mode Optimized:** If you cut 40% of variable spending (only essentials)
- **Perceived vs Actual:** Your blind spot — how much you *think* you can survive vs. reality

### Your Financial Blind Spot

The awareness gap shows the difference between your perceived runway and actual runway. If the gap is large, you're overestimating your security.

### Money Personality Archetype

ARTH.OS assigns you one of four archetypes:

| Archetype | You Tend To… | Watch Out For… |
|-----------|-------------|----------------|
| **Reactor** | Act on impulse, spend emotionally | Stress spending, social pressure |
| **Survivor** | Stay cautious, prioritize safety | Missing growth opportunities |
| **Planner** | Track commitments, plan ahead | Analysis paralysis, over-planning |
| **Builder** | Stay disciplined, focus on goals | Burnout, neglecting liquidity |

### Recommended Action

**One specific, actionable step** based on your weakest component. Examples:

- *"Use a 24-hour waiting rule for non-essential purchases this month."*
- *"Track every expense for the next 14 days (no exceptions) and total it."*
- *"Build a 1-month fixed buffer of ₹50,000 before adding discretionary savings."*

### Insight Narrative

A plain-language summary combining your score, blind spot, behavioral correlations, and risk profile.

### Decision Simulator

Test a hypothetical purchase before you make it:

1. Enter an amount (₹)
2. See: current runway → after-purchase runway
3. See: how many months of buffer you lose
4. See: risk change percentage
5. Read the personalized recommendation

**Example:** "If I buy a phone for ₹40,000, I lose 0.8 months of runway. My risk increases by 25%. Recommendation: Wait until you have at least 3 months of buffer after the purchase."

## 5. Features in Detail

### Real-Time Scoring

Every change you make to any answer instantly recalculates all scores. You don't need to submit the form to see results — the right panel updates as you type.

### Multi-Step Wizard

The 4-step flow prevents information overload. Each step focuses on one dimension of your financial health.

### Progress Persistence

Your step position and all answers are saved to your browser's local storage. Close the tab, come back tomorrow — you'll pick up where you left off.

### Behavioral Correlation Engine

ARTH.OS cross-references your answers to detect risk clusters:

- **Financial Drift Risk:** Stress/boredom spending + weak expense awareness
- **Lifestyle Inflation Risk:** Social influence + low savings
- **Debt Strain Risk:** High debt + thin cash buffer

### Post-Assessment Feedback

After completing the assessment, you'll be asked: **"What did you find most valuable?"**

Options:
- Time to Financial Crisis (survival estimate)
- Next Action to Take
- Visibility Blind Spot
- Money Archetype Profile

This feedback is anonymous and helps improve the tool.

## 6. Saving, Exporting & Resetting

### Save

Click the **Save button** (disk icon) in the top toolbar. Your assessment is saved to your browser's local storage. The save state indicator shows:
- **Ready:** No changes since last save
- **Unsaved:** You've made changes — click Save to persist
- **Saved:** Successfully stored

### Export

Click the **Export button** (download icon) to download a JSON report containing:

- All your assessment answers
- All computed results (scores, survival estimates, personality type, recommendations)
- Creation timestamp

The file is named `arth-os-financial-health-report.json` and can be opened in any text editor or imported back later.

### Reset

Click the **Reset button** (circular arrow) to clear all answers and start fresh. This:

- Clears all assessment fields to empty/default
- Removes saved state from localStorage
- Resets the wizard to Step 1
- Clears score history

**Warning:** Reset is immediate and cannot be undone. Export first if you want to keep your data.

## 7. Privacy & Your Data

### What ARTH.OS Collects (Anonymous Telemetry)

When you complete the assessment, ARTH.OS sends an **anonymous** snapshot:

- ✅ Numeric scores (health score, component scores)
- ✅ Personality type (categorical: Reactor, Planner, etc.)
- ✅ Survival estimates (months)
- ✅ Financial ratios (normalized, no raw values)
- ✅ Which component was your weakest

### What ARTH.OS NEVER Collects

- ❌ Your name, email, or phone number
- ❌ Your IP address or location
- ❌ Precise timestamps (only the date: YYYY-MM-DD)
- ❌ Your raw financial numbers (income, debt, savings amounts)
- ❌ Cookies or session identifiers

### Where Your Data Lives

- **Assessment answers:** In your browser's localStorage only (unless you click Save to server)
- **Telemetry:** In a secure Supabase database, with row-level security preventing anyone from reading individual records
- **Feedback:** In a separate table, also anonymous

### Your Control

- You can use the entire assessment without sending any telemetry — simply don't click "Finish & Review Score"
- Export your data anytime as a JSON file
- Reset clears all locally stored data

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

### "Do I need to enter exact numbers?"

No. Approximate values work. The scoring engine uses ratios and thresholds, not precise accounting. If you're unsure about your monthly expenses, use your best estimate — the awareness gap metric will reflect that uncertainty.

### "What's the difference between Fixed and Discretionary emergency savings?"

- **Fixed buffer:** Money you would *never* touch except for absolute survival (food, rent, medicine)
- **Discretionary buffer:** Extra savings that give you flexibility but could be reduced in a crisis

In crisis mode, ARTH.OS assumes you can cut 40% of variable spending. Your fixed buffer covers the remaining essentials.

### "Why does my perceived survival differ from actual?"

The awareness gap exists because people with lower financial clarity tend to overestimate their runway. ARTH.OS computes your perceived survival based on your awareness score — the less you track, the wider the gap.

### "Can I retake the assessment?"

Yes. Click **Reset** in the toolbar to start fresh. You can also just modify your existing answers — all scores update in real time.

### "Is my data shared with third parties?"

No. Telemetry data is aggregated and used only for product improvement. Individual records cannot be read by anyone (RLS policies prevent SELECT).

### "What does the Debt Repayment Rate (%) mean?"

This is the percentage of your monthly income that goes toward debt repayment. For example, if you earn ₹90,000/month and pay ₹10,800 toward debt, enter 12%.

### "Why only ONE recommended action?"

Research shows that focusing on a single high-impact action produces better follow-through than a long to-do list. If you complete the recommended action, retake the assessment to get your next priority.

### "How accurate is the Decision Simulator?"

The simulator is an estimate based on your current profile. It assumes the purchase comes from your savings. Real-world outcomes depend on factors the simulator can't see (upcoming expenses, income changes). Use it as a decision aid, not a guarantee.

### "What browsers are supported?"

All modern browsers with JavaScript enabled: Chrome 90+, Firefox 90+, Safari 15+, Edge 90+.
